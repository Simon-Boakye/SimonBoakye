About Me

A Mission Rooted in
Health Equity and Science

Simon Boakye is a Public Health Professional with over three years of experience in public health practice, driving immunization, surveillance programmes and programmatic support in Ghana. Having mastered the operational realities of last-mile delivery, I am looking to transition these field-tested experience to contribute to the mission of organization supporting programmatic and strategic efforts that protect vulnerable populations worldwide.

Currently pursuing a Master of Science in Global Health at the University of Geneva. As the Founder of EquiHealth Academy, I translate my experience into scalable leadership development. I believe that true global health security relies on resilient local systems. I contribute to systematically addressing disparities in professional opportunities by mentoring and empowering students and young professionals, especially from underserved communities. By equipping diverse, next-generation leaders with the tools to contribute to public health, I help cultivate a sustainable, self-reliant health workforce. This directly drives health equity, fosters deep community trust, and builds the localized resilience necessary to predict, prevent, and respond to global health threats.

"Geography, socioeconomic background, and access to opportunity should never determine the quality of one's contribution to global public health and human dignity."
