About Me

A Mission Rooted in
Health Equity, science-based, and Compassion

I'm Simon Boakye, a Public Health Professional with over three years of progressive experience in public health practice, specifically in immunisation campaigns, disease surveillance, outbreak response, and programme support.

Currently pursuing a Master of Science in Global Health at the University of Geneva. With a strong foundation in epidemiology, health systems, and community-driven interventions, Simon founded EquiHealth Academy to transform how the next generation of health leaders are trained and deployed and under belief that health is broader than disease treatment alone; it includes education, social support, clean environment, water and sanitation, dignity, being compassionate, and the practical skills people need to thrive in a fast-changing world. His work bridges rigorous academic research with measurable real-world impact, spanning responsible AI integration in health systems, work, scientific capacity building, and high-level partnership development with international institutions. Simon is guided by a belief that geography, socioeconomic background, and access to opportunity should never determine the quality of one's contribution to global health and human dignity.

"Geography, socioeconomic background, and access to opportunity should never determine the quality of one's contribution to global health and human dignity."

Beyond his academic and institutional work, Simon is deeply committed to mentoring early-career professionals and creating accessible pathways into global health leadership for young people from underrepresented backgrounds.
