About Me

A Mission Rooted in
Health Equity

From outbreak investigation in Accra to policy forums in Geneva — driven by a single purpose: equitable access to life-saving vaccines and health systems for all.

I'm Simon Boakye, a Public Health Professional with over three years of progressive field experience in immunisation campaigns, disease surveillance, outbreak response, and programme support. My work spans national-level EPI operations in Ghana to volunteering at the Geneva Health Forum.

Currently pursuing a Master of Science in Global Health at the Global Studies Institute, University of Geneva, I blend frontline field expertise with the analytical frameworks needed to influence global health policy.

My approach is evidence-first: whether collating AEFI data for programme adjustments, supervising national vaccination campaigns, or producing geospatial coverage maps in QGIS — I believe rigorous data is the bridge between intention and impact.
